📖 Field Guide
📊 The 4 Use Cases
UC1 Surge MomentumWhen stock rises ≥X% over N days, it reliably continues positively. Searches X=[3,5,7,10,15,20]%, N=[1-20]d, fwd=[5-60]d. Best combo per stock.
UC2 SeasonalStock goes up reliably in specific months or quarters every year. Checks all 12 months and 4 quarters with 10/20/30d hold periods.
UC3 CombinedSurge signal firing during a strong seasonal window = double confirmation. Highest quality signal.
UC4 Technical25 strategies × 300+ parameter combos: SMA/EMA cross, RSI, MACD, Bollinger, ADX, Stochastic, Williams %R, OBV, Supertrend, Double Bottom, Bull Flag, Pivot Points, ATR, Gap, Volume Breakout, Consecutive Reversal, Floor Bounce, Mean Reversion, 52W Break, Multi-Confirm, and more.
🎯 Ret%/Day — Primary Ranking Metric

A stock giving 11% in 5 days (Ret%/Day = 2.2) ranks higher than one giving 15% in 9 days (Ret%/Day = 1.67). This metric fairly compares strategies across different hold periods. Higher is always better.

🏷 Grade & Confidence System
A+ 100% confidencePattern passes ≥95% win rate in ALL validation periods: Train (2020-22), OOS-2023, OOS-2024, OOS-2025. Never broke.
A 75%+ confidencePasses 3 of 4 validation periods. Slight softening in one year but overall robust.
Confidence %% of validation periods where the pattern held ≥95% win rate. 100% = never failed in any year.
📡 Key Columns Explained
Min Ret%The LOWEST return ever seen in history. Your guaranteed floor. Every occurrence gave at least this much.
Max DD%Worst intraday dip below entry during hold. Even when negative, the final exit return is always positive (≥10% required).
RSI at SignalRSI range historically when this signal fired. Compare with current RSI — if in range, signal is in confirmed zone.
5d Before% / 20d Before%What the stock was doing BEFORE the signal. Negative = reversal signal. Positive = continuation signal.
ADXTrend strength. Above 25 = strong trend. Above 40 = very strong. Helps confirm breakout signals.
SharpeRisk-adjusted return. Above 1.0 good, above 2.0 excellent. Higher = more consistent returns relative to variability.
⚠ Rules & Caveats

Any occurrence with ≤0% return immediately invalidates the strategy for that stock. No averaging of positives and negatives. Minimum 10% return required per occurrence. 95% win rate required overall.

Entry is always next-day open after signal. Minimum 4 occurrences spanning 2+ different calendar years required before a pattern is trusted. 5 occurrences at 95% still has ~3% chance of luck — 10+ occurrences is much more reliable.

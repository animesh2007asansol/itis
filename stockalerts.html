📖 Guide tabs · columns · abbreviations
Tab Guide
Today AlertsGreen badge=today. Amber=older (run workflow). Strategy fired on latest date. Empty=no signal, check Still Valid.
Still ValidPast signals in tenure, price ge5% below min target. Still good to buy. Min Target=entry*(1+min_ret%). Sorted biggest gap first.
Signal HistoryAll historical trades. OPEN=tenure not expired. ~ prefix=partial return (today price, not final). Days in cal (calendar days). hold_days in td (trading days). 20td approx 30cal.
UC1 SurgeUse Case 1: Surge Momentum. Large rise over N days then further gains. Views: All/Today/Active.
UC2 SeasonalUse Case 2: Seasonal. Rises in specific month every year. If signal says June: wait for June 1st! See Trading Calendar.
UC3 CombinedUse Case 3: Combined. UC1 and UC2 same day = double confirmation. Highest quality.
UC4 TechnicalUse Case 4: Technical. 25 strategies: SMA Cross, RSI Oversold, MACD, Bollinger, Volume Breakout, ATR Expansion, Double Bottom, Bull Flag, Pivot Bounce, Supertrend, ADX, Williams %R, OBV, Stochastic, and more. 300+ parameter combos.
Long-Term (LT)Top 40 for multi-week holds. Grade A/A+, Confidence ge70%. Green=today signal. Amber=active (days+gap shown). Grey=watch.
Trading CalendarMonthly view: UC2 stocks by buy month, current month first (green). Conditions Tracker: stocks by conditions in range. 3 dots=all 3=signal imminent.
Buying StrategyEntry day stats. Gap%=open vs prev close. Dip%=intraday drop from open. D1 Close%=entry close vs signal close. Recommendation=buy at Open or wait for dip.
Metrics - Full Names
Ret%/DayReturn Percent Per Day = Avg Ret% divided by Hold td. Primary ranking. 11%/5td=2.2/day beats 15%/9td=1.67/day.
Min/Avg/Max Ret%Min/Average/Max Return Percent. Min=floor (always ge10%). Avg=typical. Max=best ever.
Hold tdHold Trading Days. 5td=approx 1 week, 20td=approx 1 month, 60td=approx 3 months. NOT calendar days.
Win%Win Rate Percent. Trades returning ge10% / total. Must be ge95%. Any negative return makes strategy rejected.
Conf%Confidence Percent. Out-of-sample years (2023/2024/2025) with ge95% win rate. 100%=A+, 75%=A, 50%=B.
Grade A+/A/BA Plus=passed all OOS years. A=passed 75%+. B=passed 50% (use with caution).
SharpeSharpe Ratio. Risk-adjusted return. Above 1.0 good, above 2.0 excellent.
Max DD%Maximum Drawdown Percent. Worst intraday dip below entry. Still exited profitable despite dip.
LT ScoreLong-Term Score 0-100. Trend above EMA50, 60d return, A+ strategies, return rate.
OOS Ret%Out-of-Sample Return Percent. On 2023-2025 data NOT used for training. Proves pattern is real.
RSIRelative Strength Index (14d). Below 30=oversold. Above 70=overbought. At Signal=range when pattern fired.
Vol RatioVolume Ratio = today vol divided by 20d avg. 2x=double normal activity.
ADXAverage Directional Index. Trend strength only (not direction). Above 25 strong, above 40 very strong.
EMA/SMAExponential/Simple Moving Average. EMA weights recent prices more. Fast cross above slow = bullish.
MACDMoving Average Convergence Divergence = EMA12 minus EMA26. Cross above 9d signal line = buy signal.
BB (Bollinger Bands)Bollinger Bands = 20d mean plus/minus 2 StdDev. Lower touch=oversold. Narrow width=squeeze before breakout.
ATRAverage True Range (14d). Avg daily volatility. Range above 2x ATR = breakout.
5d/20d Before%5-Day/20-Day Before Percent. Return in 5 or 20 trading days BEFORE signal. Negative=was falling before reversal.
cal / tdcal = calendar days (all days including weekends). td = trading days (market open only). 20td is approximately 30cal. Signal History shows cal days for elapsed and remaining.
~ (tilde) prefixPartial/open trade return. Today price used as exit. Hold period not over. Final return may differ.
Gap% / Dip% / D1 Close%Buying Strategy tab. Gap%=(Day1 Open - Signal Day Close)/Signal Day Close. Dip%=(Day1 Low - Day1 Open)/Day1 Open. D1 Close%=(Day1 Close - Signal Day Close)/Signal Day Close.
Key Rules

td vs cal: Hold Days are in td (trading days). Calendar days elapsed will always be larger than td value. 20td hold = approx 30cal tenure. Days Past capped at tenure so never exceeds it. Correct behavior.

~ prefix = partial return: Open trade return uses today price as exit. Trade still running.

UC2 Seasonal: If signal says Buy June 1st, do nothing today in April. Mark calendar for June and check Trading Calendar tab.

📖 How This System Works

This system runs a 4-stage quantitative analysis on every NSE stock with daily turnover >Rs 5 Cr and price >Rs 10, using 5 years of historical data.

Stage 1 — Discovery
12 technical strategies are computed on every stock using vectorised pandas: SMA Cross, EMA Bounce, RSI Oversold, MACD Cross, Bollinger Band Bounce, Volume Breakout, Consecutive Reversal, Floor Bounce, Momentum Gap, Range Expand, Trend Pullback, 52-Week High Break.
Stage 2 — Backtest
Every strategy is backtested on the full 5-year history. Entry = next-day open after signal. Exit = close after 5 or 20 days. Only strategies with ≥60% win rate AND ≥1% avg return AND ≥5 trades are kept. Win rate, avg return, max drawdown, Sharpe ratio, profit factor all computed.
Stage 3 — Challenge (Out-of-Sample Verification)
Data is split into two halves. Best strategies are re-tested on EACH half separately. A+ grade = passes both halves. A grade = passes one half. B grade = discarded. This prevents curve-fitting — you only see strategies that work on unseen data.
Stage 4 — Daily Alerts + Long-Term Picks
Each morning after data update: stocks where a verified strategy signals today appear as alerts. Long-term picks are A/A+ stocks with strong uptrend and score ≥50. Checkpoint system ensures only new data is re-analysed — the system gets faster over time.
Important Notes

All analysis is based purely on price and volume data. Entry is always next-day open (never today's close). Hold periods are 5 days (short-term) or 20 days (medium-term). Past performance does not guarantee future results — but A+ grade means the pattern survived on data the model never trained on.

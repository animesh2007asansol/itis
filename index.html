Hammer / Pin Bar Pattern
A bullish reversal signal. Sellers drove price far down intraday, but buyers pushed it back up by close — showing buying strength at lower levels.
✅Lower wick ≥ 2× body — strong intraday rejection of lows
✅Upper wick ≤ 25% of body — minimal selling at the top
✅Body > 0 — must have a real Open vs Close difference
📐Body = |Close − Open| | Lower wick = min(O,C) − Low | Upper wick = High − max(O,C)
How to use: When you see a signal in the Alerts tab, the stock rejected lower prices strongly that day. Watch for continuation upward on the next trading session. Works best after a pullback or at key support. Always confirm with volume — higher volume on the signal day = stronger confirmation.
